# Lumen TV: guide for AI assistants

The full guide is [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md): read it first and follow it (it is written for any assistant, not only
Claude Code), with [SETUP.md](SETUP.md) as the steps the person sees. Most people ask in plain words, like "Set up
Lumen TV on my TV at 192.168.1.50. I live in Jaipur.", and don't know what a terminal is: explain each step simply,
and ask before anything that changes their TV or computer.
